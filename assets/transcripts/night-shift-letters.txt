Letters from the Night Shift
StoryCast transcript
========================================

At three in the morning, the hospital corridor is a quiet river.
Dara walks it every night with a cart of folded notes.
Patients write to family they cannot see until sunrise.
He reads each one aloud, softly, for those who cannot read for themselves.
A tired father. A girl who wants her cat to know she is brave.
He never keeps a copy, because the words belong to the people who wrote them.
When the sun comes up, the notes are gone, and the corridor is loud again.
