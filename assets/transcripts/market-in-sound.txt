A Market in Sound
StoryCast transcript
========================================

[Visual description: A dark indigo screen with the title in white. An amber sound wave pulses along the lower half of the frame in time with the narration.]

Before the first customer arrives, the market already has a voice.
Metal shutters rattle upward, one stall at a time.
A radio hums near the fruit sellers, tuned to a station nobody chose.
Then the bargaining begins, and the whole place becomes one long conversation.
Prices rise and fall like a melody that only the regulars can hum.
For Amina, who has sold tomatoes here for thirty years, these sounds are a map.
She tells the time by the clink of the scales, and the weather by the pitch of the crowd.
Last year she lost most of her sight, but she never lost her stall.
She says listening is just looking through a different door.
So we asked her to describe the market, and we recorded every word.
This story is for anyone who has ever found their way by sound.
