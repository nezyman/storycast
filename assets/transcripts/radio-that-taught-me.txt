The Radio That Taught Me to Listen
StoryCast transcript
========================================

My grandfather owned one radio, and it was a better teacher than any I had.
Every evening he turned the dial slowly, like opening a safe.
Static, then a song, then a stranger reading the news in a language I barely knew.
He never explained. He only said, wait for it.
That is how I learned that listening is a skill, and patience is part of the signal.
Now I record other people's evenings, so someone else can wait for it too.
