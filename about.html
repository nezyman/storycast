About & access

Stories are better when everyone can get in.

StoryCast treats access as part of the story, not an add-on. Here is what we promise, how to use it, and how to tell us when we fall short.

Our mission

To publish short audio and video stories that anyone can hear, watch or read, on any device, with any assistive technology.

A recording that only works if you can see the screen, or only if you can hear the speaker, leaves people out. So every StoryCast story ships with captions or a transcript from day one, and the pages around it are built to work with a keyboard, a screen reader and a zoomed-in browser.

The launch stories on this site are fictional pieces written for this project. The narration is a synthetic demo voice.

Accessibility features

  • Captions

    Video includes English captions in WebVTT, switched on by default. Turn them off from the player if you prefer.

  • Transcripts

    Every audio and video story has a full text transcript on the page, plus a plain-text download. Video transcripts include a description of the visuals.

  • Keyboard friendly

    Everything works without a mouse, focus is always visible, and a skip link jumps past the navigation.

  • Readable colour

    Text and controls meet WCAG 2.1 AA contrast. Links are underlined and the current page is marked by more than colour.

  • Clear structure

    Landmarks, one top-level heading per page and a logical heading order help screen reader users move around quickly.

  • Flexible layouts

    Layouts reflow when you zoom or use larger text, and the story cards adapt to the space they are given.

How to engage with a story

  1. Pick a format. Play the audio or video, or go straight to the transcript.
  2. Follow along. On the story page, the line being spoken is highlighted in the transcript.
  3. Jump around. Select any timestamp to replay from that moment.
  4. Take it with you. Download the transcript as plain text to read offline or in your own reader.

Keyboard guide

Keys that work on StoryCast pages
Key What it does
Tab / Shift + Tab Move forward or back through links, players and transcript timestamps. The first Tab on any page reveals the skip link.
Enter Follow a link or press a button, including a transcript timestamp.
Space Play or pause a focused player. Player shortcuts vary slightly between browsers.

Accessibility statement

StoryCast aims to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. This statement was last reviewed on 17 October 2026.

Known limitations

  • Video visuals are described in text, in the caption area and the transcript. There is no separate audio-described soundtrack yet.
  • Player controls come from your browser, so their look and shortcuts differ between browsers.
  • Captions are in English only.

Report a barrier

If something here does not work for you, tell us what you were trying to do and what got in the way. Email access@storycast.example and we will aim to reply within five working days.
